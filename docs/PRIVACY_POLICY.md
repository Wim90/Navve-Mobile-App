# Navve Privacy Policy

**Last Updated: October 12, 2026**

Your privacy is important to us. This policy explains how Navve handles your data. The short version: we don't collect, sell, or share your personal information.

---

## 1. What We Don't Collect

Navve is designed with privacy in mind. We don't collect:

- Your personal information (name, email, phone number)
- Your location or movement patterns
- Analytics or usage data
- Device identifiers or tracking data
- Any information about the places you save

**We can't read your data. Period.** That includes the places, collections, and trip plans you choose to share, which are encrypted before they leave your phone.

---

## 2. Where Your Data Lives

**iCloud is enabled by default, but you can turn it off.**

**If you use iCloud** (iCloud is on by default; you can disable it in Settings → Apple ID → iCloud → Navve):

- **Free Plan**: Up to 8 places backed up to your iCloud
- **Pro Plan**: Unlimited places synced via your iCloud (one-time purchase)
- Navve doesn't run any servers, and we never receive your places
- If you choose to share a place, a collection, or a trip plan, the encrypted information you share is stored by Apple in iCloud so the person you share with can add it to their own Navve (see Section 3)
- Only you can access your places through your Apple ID unless you decide to share them with another person.
- Your data is encrypted by Apple's iCloud

*Think of it this way: your places are stored on your device and in your iCloud, just like your photos or notes. We never see them.*

**If you do not use iCloud** (you have disabled iCloud for Navve in your device settings):

- All your places and collections are stored **only on your device**
- Nothing is synced to the cloud; we have no copy of your data
- **Deleting the app will delete all of this data permanently.** It cannot be recovered or retrieved

---

## 3. Sharing Places, Collections, and Trip Plans

You can share a place, a collection, or a trip plan using Messages (iMessage or text message) or AirDrop. Here is exactly what happens:

- When you tap Share, Navve encrypts the place, collection, or trip plan on your phone.
- The encrypted information you share is saved in iCloud, which Apple operates. Navve doesn't run any servers.
- What you share can only be opened by the people you send it to. The code that unlocks it travels with what you send, and we never receive it, so we can't read what you share.
- Anyone who receives what you share can add it to their own Navve. Only send it to people you trust, as you would a private message.
- **Included:** the names, addresses, categories, phone numbers, and websites of the places. For collections and trip plans, also the name, description, cover photo, and for trip plans the dates, days, and time slots.
- **Never included:** your personal notes, favorites, and visited status.
- What you share is available for 30 days. After that, no one can add it to their Navve. The encrypted information is deleted from iCloud the next time you open Navve with an internet connection. Until then it stays in iCloud, and no one can read it without the code you sent.
- To share a place, a collection, or a trip plan, you need to be signed in to iCloud. To add to your own Navve the places someone shared with you, you don't.

---

## 4. Apple Services We Use

Navve uses Apple's services to function properly:

- **iCloud** (for sharing): When you share, Navve saves the encrypted information you share in iCloud (see Section 3).
- **iCloud** (optional): If you use it, iCloud backs up and syncs your places across your devices. You can turn this off in your device settings (Settings → Apple ID → iCloud → Navve); in that case, data stays only on the device (see Section 2).
- **MapKit**: Shows places on a map and provides geocoding
- **CoreLocation**: Gets your location (only when you use map features)
- **StoreKit**: Manages in-app purchases (Pro one-time purchase) and offer code redemption (gift cards, promotional codes) through the App Store

These services are governed by Apple's Privacy Policy. We don't receive any data from these services.

---

## 5. Location Permissions

Navve may request location access for these features:

- Showing your current location on the map
- Sorting places by distance from you
- Centering the map on your location

*Your location is processed on your device only. We never receive, store, or track your location.*

---

## 6. Third-Party Services

When you use certain features, you may interact with third-party services:

- Apple Maps, Google Maps, or Waze (when getting directions)
- Your email app (when contacting support)
- Messages and AirDrop (when you share places)

These services have their own privacy policies. We don't control or receive data from them.

---

## 7. Your Data, Your Control

You have complete control over your data:

- Export all your places anytime (Settings → Export Places)
- Delete individual places or all places
- **Choose whether to use iCloud**: Disable iCloud for Navve in your device settings (Settings → Apple ID → iCloud) if you prefer to keep data only on your device
- Delete the app to remove all local data
- What you share can be added to someone's Navve for 30 days

**If you use iCloud:** Deleting the app does not delete your data from iCloud. You can manage or delete that data in Settings → Apple ID → iCloud.

**If you do not use iCloud:** All data is only on your device. Deleting the app deletes that data permanently and it cannot be retrieved. Export your places first if you want to keep a copy.

---

## 8. Children's Privacy

Navve doesn't collect any personal information from anyone, including children under 13. The app is safe for all ages.

---

## 9. Data Security

**When you use iCloud:** Your data is protected by Apple's iCloud (encrypted in transit and at rest, accessible only with your Apple ID; two-factor authentication is recommended).

**When you don't use iCloud:** Your data stays on your device and is protected by your device's security (e.g. passcode, Face ID, or Touch ID). We never receive or store it.

**When you share:** What you share is encrypted on your phone. The code that unlocks it travels only with what you send.
